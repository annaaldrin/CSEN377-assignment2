# CSEN377-assignment2
Interactive data visualization for EV sales in CA counties over time (2008-2025).

Based on my observations from the data, this assignment and last assignment, I wanted to see if the number of EV sales per county was reflective of respective affluence and social status there. Due to the rise of Teslas, green energy, and overall reputation, EV’s are now booming in popularity with companies like Waymo slowly reshaping the car landscape. Because of this, I sought to use my interactive visualization to see and reflect this hypothesis, that EV use is particularly popular amongst those in high-income, high-status counties. Based on our labs and activities in class, I wanted to create something that reflected what we had learned: a bar chart, Chloropleth-inspired map, and a line graph.
